About Us

We exist to bring inspiration and innovation to every athlete in the world

Nike, Inc. is more than a footwear and apparel company. We are a brand driven by a passion for sport, creativity, and innovation.


Founded in 1964, Nike has grown from a small company into one of the world's leading sports brands. We create innovative footwear, apparel, and equipment designed to help athletes perform at their best.


We believe that sport has the power to inspire people, bring communities together, and push the world forward. Our goal is to continue creating products and experiences that empower every athlete to reach their full potential.
